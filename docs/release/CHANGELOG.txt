STBC Voice Crew – Changelog

1.1.0 (2026-10-28)
Highlights: the First Officer and the open source release on GitHub.
- The Phuntasy Productions logo appears briefly at game start, before the game's own logos ([Allgemein] LogoBeimStart = false turns it off).
- New: French voice commands for the bot crew (Vosk model downloaded on first use, [Sprache] BefehlsSprache = fr or
  follows the game language).
- Multiplayer: a banner at the top of the desktop screen shows who hosts and whether the mod is active there (green: you
  host, yellow: the host has the mod, red: the host has no mod – no voice commands).
- Readme, changelog and licenses now come in BepInEx\plugins\STBCVoiceCrew; in the game folder the changelog
  overwrote BepInEx's changelog.txt.
- Package: includes BepInEx.cfg with the entry point the game needs (fresh installs started with a black screen).
  The mod also repairs this setting itself if it is missing (when started via Steam already before the game window opens).
- New: First Officer ("Number One") – an AI first officer without a language model, always on the bridge and in the lobby.
  - Advises a human captain: warnings when shields are down under fire, the hull is critical, a friendly ship is
    targeted or the warp destination leads away from the mission; hints when the mission stalls or nothing happens.
  - Knows the mission flow: rules derived from the game's own mission scripts (campaign and all random missions) –
    distances, "scan only", "don't destroy", "knock their shields down before beaming", safe distances, escapes,
    particle gathering, silent running, energy waves.
  - Takes command when nobody sits in the captain's chair: reads the objectives, sets the warp destination, gives
    orders to the bot crew and to human officers (spoken and on their console), waits for "Aye", reminds, praises.
    Orders come one at a time: he speaks, the crew executes and answers, then the next order follows.
    Before warping near an enemy he gives full thrust for a few seconds and holds fire, then jumps while moving.
  - Speaks only when nobody else is talking (crew, game voices, briefing, players).
  - Rare Star Trek puns on the bridge and in the lobby.
  - "Number One, take the bridge" / "You are in command": you stay in the captain's chair and watch, he runs the ship.
    [FirstOfficer] AutoKommando = true does this at every mission start.
  - Voice commands: "Number One / Commander, suggestions?", "You have the bridge", "I have the bridge",
    "Number One, dismissed" / "Number One, to the bridge", "Number One, tell me a joke". German: "Nummer Eins / Commander, Vorschlag?",
    "Sie haben die Brücke", "Ich habe die Brücke", "Nummer Eins, wegtreten", "Nummer Eins, erzähl einen Witz".
  - English, German and French lines; voices by Piper TTS (downloaded on first use).
  - On/off: overlay button, F6, voice command, or [FirstOfficer] Aktiv in the config.
  - Mission execution: warp with alignment wait and coil recharge, breaks away from nearby enemies first, impulse to
    places named in the objectives, scans directly via the tactical scanner (real range and progress), stops on arrival,
    attacks the nearest enemy for 'eliminate' objectives, picks a system from 'Warp to X, Y, or Z'.
- Test mode for unattended runs ([Test] AutoTest = zufall/kampagne): selects mouse/keyboard, starts solo games,
  the First Officer takes the bridge, screenshots in BepInEx\plugins\STBCVoiceCrew\test.
- Open source (MIT) on GitHub. Three ways to install: setup EXE (finds the game in all Steam libraries or Ubisoft
  Connect, installs and updates), a one-line PowerShell command, or the ZIP.
- Much smaller download: speech models, Piper and voices are downloaded by the mod on first use for the game language.
- The overlay shows when a newer version is available ([Allgemein] UpdatePruefen).
- Plugin folder renamed to BepInEx\plugins\STBCVoiceCrew; the old StbcAiCrew folder is taken over and removed.
- Plugin id and DLL renamed to de.phuntasyproductions.stbcvoicecrew; the settings of 1.0 are taken over.
- Multiplayer: runs on the host only, speaks through the game's voice chat; players without the mod are unaffected.

1.0.0
- First public release.
- Offline voice commands (Vosk) in English and German, replacing the defunct IBM Watson service.
- Bot crew (Rao, Hoyle, T'nar) executes orders with their original voices; every human on the bridge may command,
  multiplayer voice chat is understood on the host.
- Targets by name or from the console of the person giving the order; friendly ships are only fired upon when named.
- Warp/impulse sequences, transporter lowers shields first, standing orders respected by crew reflexes.
- Experimental manoeuvres (avoid hazards, keep distance, circle, flee, stay out of enemy sensor range).
- Command language: game language, English, German or both at once (learned per speaker).
- Overlay in German when the game is set to German, otherwise in English.
