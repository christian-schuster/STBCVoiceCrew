[size=5][b]STBC Voice Crew[/b][/size]
[i]Voice commands are back – fully local, no Watson, no internet.[/i]

Since IBM shut down Watson, Star Trek: Bridge Crew has no voice commands. This mod brings them back with a fast
offline speech recognizer (Vosk). The bot crew – Rao, Hoyle and T'nar – follows your spoken orders in about
0.3 seconds and answers with their original voices.

[b]New in 1.1: the First Officer – and the mod is now open source (MIT) on GitHub.[/b]

[b]Focus: single player.[/b] The mod is made first of all for playing alone with the bot crew. It also works in multiplayer (install it on the host), and the game stays fully compatible with installations without the mod – players who don't have it can still play with you.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Voice command languages: English, German and French
[*]CPU only, no graphics card, no account, no cloud
[*]Every human on the bridge can give orders (multiplayer: install on the host)
[*]Targets by name ("Scan the Ragusa") or the target marked on your console
[*]Warp and impulse sequences, "Energize" lowers the shields first
[*]Standing orders the reflexes respect: "Power down all systems so they can't detect us", "Hold your fire", "Keep the shields down"
[*]Experimental manoeuvres: "Fly around the minefield", "Keep ten kilometers distance", "Circle the station", "Get us out of here"
[/list]

[b]New in 1.1: First Officer ("Number One")[/b]
An AI first officer who is always on the bridge and in the lobby – no language model, no internet.
[list]
[*]Advises you as captain: warnings when something is dangerous or wrong, hints when the mission stalls – he knows the mission flow from the game's own mission scripts (campaign and random missions)
[*]Takes command when the captain's chair is empty: sets the warp destination, gives orders to bots and human officers and waits for "Aye"
[*]Speaks only when nobody else does; now and then a Star Trek pun
[*]"Number One / Commander, suggestions?", "You have the bridge", "I have the bridge", "Number One, dismissed"
[*]Speaks English, German and French, voiced with Piper TTS (downloaded on first use)
[/list]

[b]Fully automated testing with Claude Code[/b]
A test mode starts solo missions on its own and lets the First Officer play them, with log lines and screenshots. Large parts of the mod were developed this way with Claude Code: it starts the game, watches the runs, fixes what goes wrong and starts the next run – unattended.

[b]Installation[/b]
Unpack into the game folder (where stbc.exe is). BepInEx 5 is included. Start the game – the overlay in the top left of the desktop monitor shows the status (in English, or German if the game is set to German). In VR you get feedback through beeps and the crew's answers.
F4 = push to talk, F5 = mute microphone, F6 = First Officer on/off, F7 = overlay.

[b]Tips[/b]
[list]
[*]Short orders work without addressing anyone ("Shields up"). For longer ones say "Helm, …", "Tactical, …", "Engineering, …" or hold F4.
[*]If your microphone name contains umlauts (ä, ö, ü), rename it in Windows – the game cannot use it otherwise.
[*]Settings: BepInEx\config\de.phuntasyproductions.stbcvoicecrew.cfg (language: spiel/de/en/fr/auto).
[/list]

[b]Known limitations[/b]
Warp/impulse destinations are still picked on the map. Hails, probes and cancelling red alert stay at the captain's console.
